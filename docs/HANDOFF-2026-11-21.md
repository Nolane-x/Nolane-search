## Telegram and file-storage model

- Worker/D1 store job metadata, bounded summaries/progress/traces, pending selections, workflow definitions and expiring references; uploaded file bytes are not stored in D1.
- Telegram uploads are downloaded by GitHub Actions to an ephemeral runner. Inputs, intermediate results and final artifacts remain on that runner; files are not committed to Git or retained as permanent workflow artifacts.
- Final output is sent to Telegram. Successful jobs clear the source-file reference; failed jobs may retain it for retry while Telegram still exposes the file.
- Retry downloads the original again and reruns the full pipeline; it does not resume from intermediate artifacts. Job metadata/history is bounded and expires under the operations policy (up to 30 days).
- Optional Telegram archive channel stores snapshots of monitored website content on first capture and later changes. D1 stores a compact extract; the archive is not an unlimited searchable database.
- Browser chat transcripts are not persisted as server-side chat records; recent browser history is bounded client-side.
- CI, health checks and webhook verification do not replace owner-only end-to-end testing of a real Telegram upload → runner → delivered output, retry, archive-channel delivery and callback buttons.
